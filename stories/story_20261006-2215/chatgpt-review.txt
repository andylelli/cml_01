What works
The opening is much smoother. The fog over Halloway Manor, the circus in the stables, the sawdust mingling with manor austerity, and the isolated 1953 setting all give the story a clearer identity before the body is even examined.    Pasted text
The murder setup is clean: Cecil is found behind the writing desk, stabbed with the letter opener, the clock stopped at quarter past nine, and the hinge/pivot clue under the carpet appears early. That gives the reader a proper sealed-room puzzle from Chapter 1.    Pasted text
The physical mechanism remains strong. The fourteen-inch carpet disturbance, ninety-degree swing, and three-foot hidden passage are clear and easy to visualize. This is still a better core mechanism than the fog-hotel draft because it has one central physical trick rather than many smaller proof-pieces.    Pasted text
Main issue: Ivor is too obvious too early
By Chapter 2, the story has already given us:
Ivor at the library mechanism,
Ivor handling estate plans,
Ivor’s prints on the letter opener,
the library-to-victim-room route,
Adela saying he had means and opportunity.    Pasted text

That is logical, but it weakens suspense. The reader is likely to think: “It’s Ivor” by Chapter 2.
A better structure would be:
Chapter 2: reveal the library route and missing letter opener, but not the fingerprint match yet.
Chapter 5: reveal the clock lie.
Chapter 6: reveal the carpet wear/panel geometry.
Chapter 8: reveal Ivor’s fingerprints and practiced operation together.

The fingerprint match should arrive later. Right now it almost solves the case immediately.
Reveal scene: good, but less theatrical than the previous version
Chapter 8 works. Adela opens the panel, Harriet cannot operate it, Ivor can, and the letter opener prints point to him.    Pasted text
But it would be stronger if Adela did not open the panel first. Let the suspects try before the mechanism is fully demonstrated:

Marguerite failed.
Ottoline could identify the seam but not the catch.

That proves Ivor’s knowledge more dramatically. In the current version, Adela opens it first, which slightly weakens the “Ivor alone knew” moment.    Pasted text
Clock logic is improved
The clock clue is clearer here. The clock is manually halted at 9:15, but the fire was stoked at 9:30, tea remained warm, and the later evidence points to a true murder window nearer 9:45.    Pasted text

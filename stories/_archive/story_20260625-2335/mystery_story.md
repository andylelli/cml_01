## Chapter 10: The Revelation

The storm had passed, leaving a pale daylight that crept through the tall windows, glinting on the polished silver and the scattered remains of the night's turmoil. In a remembered moment, Eleanor Voss stood at the head of the long table, her notebook closed, her gloved hands resting lightly on the wood.

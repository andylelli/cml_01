## Chapter 7: Secrets Beneath the Surface

## Chapter 8: The Discriminating Test

There was a hush broken only by the whisper of late-night wind against the windowpane and the faint, metallic creak of gears as Eleanor Voss set her gloved hand to the great clock's casing.

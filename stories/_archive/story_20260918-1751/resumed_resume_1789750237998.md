## Chapter 1: Discovery

Blunt force-paperweight, no doubt." He placed the evidence in a clean handkerchief, the ornate crest glinting beneath the dark stain. "There was a struggle, perhaps?"

"You said yesterday that Montague was due on the beach by nine o'clock. The judge himself claims he saw Montague crossing the sand at precisely that time, compass in hand."

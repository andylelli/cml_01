## Chapter 7: Uncovering Secrets

Hugo's unique capability and motive to tamper with the clock had been laid bare, and with it, the comfortable certainties of the day were swept away. The truth, however uncomfortable, was no longer a matter of trusting the loudest chime, but of seeing who had set it ringing in the first place.

## Chapter 8: The Final Trap: Discriminating Test

Outside, the wind picked up, rattling the windowpanes-a reminder that the storm, both within and without, was far from over. ## Chapter 7: Secrets Uncovered

Sylvia Trent's eyes narrowed, her voice icy. 'Mallory never could resist holding power over people. She enjoyed it-making us dance to her tune. ' She turned to Eleanor, her composure slipping. Cold enough to keep a man's head clear, and no one to vouch for me but the gulls. ' He let that settle before his voice dropped lower, rougher. 'She knew everyone's secrets.

' The silence that followed was dense, each guest measuring the others with new suspicion. Eleanor's breath caught as she felt how tangled the web had become. The letter had not clarified the picture; it had only muddied the waters further. Now each guest had a plausible grievance, a reason to resent Dr. Finch. She glanced at the clock, its hands still fixed at ten minutes past nine, and felt the weight of uncertainty settle over her shoulders. Somewhere inside the casing the mechanism ticked on, faint and stubborn, a reminder that the truth, whatever it was, remained just out of reach.

## Chapter 8: The Pattern Emerges

## Chapter 9: The Final Trap

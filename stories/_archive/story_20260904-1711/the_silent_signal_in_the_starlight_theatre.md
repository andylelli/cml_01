## Chapter 8: The Final Trap: Discriminating Test

" She tapped the approval entry, where Harriet Whitlock's signature sat beside the smudged ink. "Yet the doctor's report estimates death at seventeen minutes past seven.

That's before the vocal cue was called. "The paper texture here is newer, rougher than the earlier pages. The ink bled across the margin, handled in haste-distinct from the rest. The timeline contradiction is clear: Harriet Whitlock was already dead before the official rehearsal began. ## Chapter 10: The Cost of Authority

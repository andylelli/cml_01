Biggest issue

The bell-delay mechanism needs a clearer explanation.

Right now, the story says:

the bell is struck
a muffling pad creates a delay
the echo chamber is six feet deep
the chime is heard seconds later

The idea is good, but mechanically it is vague. A leather pad would normally muffle or soften a sound, not delay it by ten minutes. The story seems to mix two ideas:

A muffled immediate strike that people fail to hear clearly.
A delayed chime that rings later.

Those are not the same mechanism.

The clean fix is to define exactly how the trick works:

Charles struck Lady Beatrice with the clapper at 7:10.
He fixed a leather pad to hold the clapper away from the bell and attached a delayed release cord.
At 7:20, the cord released the clapper, producing the chime.
The household heard the chime and assumed the fatal blow happened then.
The stopped clock at 7:30 was staged afterward to reinforce the false sequence.

Or simpler:

The bell did not delay naturally. Charles staged a second, delayed strike. The leather pad muffled the first fatal impact, and the released clapper produced the later chime.

That would make the mechanism far more believable.

acoustic delay: seconds

# 07 — DEPTH FROM WHAT THE PIPELINE ALREADY WROTE

**2026-09-25. £0 to build; unverified until a paid run.** Code: `packages/prose-engine/src/depth.ts`.

## The finding — MEASURED on the four golden cases

Of ~41,000 words of character, place, period and relationship material the upstream agents write,
about **12%** reached v2's writer. What reached it: persona, secret, stakes, speech (100%), part of the
relationships. What did not (0%): profile paragraphs (6,286 w), location paragraphs and time-of-day
sensory variants (~4,500 w), character portraits and era intersection, the era's register and physical
constraints, each character's internal conflict and personal stake, the emotional arc.

## The rule for bringing it in

Each piece is **owned by one chapter** and asked for **as an act**, in that chapter's contract only —
never the bible, which every chapter call reads (the 69's "harsh winters", WP-001 §4.3). Nothing goes
to the reveal or aftermath except a sensory note.

| item | source | on the page as |
|---|---|---|
| place | the key location matching the scene (a shared distinctive word), its variant for the scene's hour and the book's weather; else one note of the setting's palette | one sight and one sound, each once in the book |
| access | the location's `accessControl`, first chapter there | a rule somebody runs up against |
| period | `historicalMoment.physicalConstraints`, one per chapter from ch 2; absences ("No television") skipped | a thing that happens to somebody |
| inner life | each living non-culprit's `internalConflict` + `personalStakeInCase`, first chapter they are on the page | one choice that shows what pulls them two ways |
| relationship | a pair with a `sharedHistory`, both on the page, once per pair; the history is removed from the bible for that pair; "histories" that are the investigation skipped | one exchange that carries it and names nothing |

The culprit's internal conflict is never asked for: written for the murderer it is often the motive in
other words.

**Withdrawn — the profile paragraphs.** Read on the golden cases, they re-word the case fields
("His alibi, managing the stables alone, was a fragile shield") and carry the humour label P4.2 removed
("his speech was laced with polite savagery"). WP-001 §2.2 counted one dated origin in 378 profiles.
Mining them would put labels back, not depth.

## Measured on the golden contracts

Chapters before the reveal carrying texture: ≥ 7 of 8 in each case; ~170–200 words of contract per
chapter at most; no piece given twice (test). Fixed wording of the new lines is in `TEMPLATE`, so the
echo checker catches it if printed.

Consequences in the Lounge

Night soaked the seaside hotel lounge, deepening every contour with layers of shadow that curved across the threadbare carpet and sunken armchairs. Eleanor Voss hovered in the threshold, her fingertips cold, nerves humming from the confrontation in the clock room. The oppressive tension that had ruled for days had ebbed, but the silence in its wake felt brittle, as if one word might shatter it. Hugo Vane crouched in a battered armchair, knuckles pressed white against his knees, attention locked on the worn carpet. Captain Ivor Hale stood beside the hearth, posture rigid, features set in a mask that finally seemed to be failing, his uniform dull in the lamplight. The smell of salt and old tea clung to everything. Eleanor stepped into the circle of survivors.

## What the pair settled

**The owner's points 1–4 are delivered on the page.** B's chapter 1 opens on two paragraphs of the hotel and the cliff
path built from the location profile (whitewash, mullioned windows, slate roof, the winding road, the iron sign, gulls);
names the victim with his job and age; introduces all five living people with their job and what they were to him;
has somebody ask for the police and a doctor, and the telephone dead; and puts no joke at the body. A′ does none of it.
The leaked instruction phrases are gone. All of it from one paid redo, and the writer opened on narration the first time
it was told to (0 of 25 before, never told).

**Point 5 got worse, and the cause is ours.** B's pre-editor draft says "pressed her hand against the" **32** times (A′:
0) and "pressed" in any form 160 times (A′: 22), growing from 6 in chapter 1 to 26 in chapter 10. Among sentences that
directly follow a spoken line, the openings collapse: A′ "asked 25, nodded 11, reached 10, replied 9, glanced 6, smiled
5"; B "asked 39, **pressed 35**, replied 9, moved 6". Nothing in B's prompt says "press". **INFERRED** cause: D5 removed
the examples from the brief's exchange line — "what somebody ELSE does: a movement, an object handled, a look away, the
next question" became "belongs to somebody else, and is something they do" — and the writer filled the slot with one
default gesture, which the book-so-far then compounded. The examples were diversifying the act; they leaked only mildly
(the specimen audit: "next question" 8 uses in 10 books). One pair cannot prove the cause; the reverted wording in the
next pair can.

**The tail finding was mostly undone by a guard.** 113 `body_tail` findings went to the editor; 107 edits applied in all,
and 53 rolled back — **45 by `registerNotWorse`**. Cutting ", her fingers steady on the desk" makes the sentence score
higher on the machine-register instrument, whose slope against the reads is zero since 1 September (WP-006 §3.2). The
guard reverts the one edit this finding asks for.

**Three costs of the opening, by reading.** (1) The introductions arrive as a roll call: five consecutive paragraphs open
"Name, appositive, verb", and Eleanor's appositive is the contract's text nearly word for word. (2) The death lines are
mostly stock (Charles: "I'm probably making too much of it"). (3) "No signal" — a mobile-phone phrase in 1934.

**Do not send B to a reader.** Its SHIP-CHECK says WORTH A LOOK (61.2 per 10k, worst span "she pressed her hand against
the" ×7), and CLAUDE.md forbids it until a matched pair clears it.

## A side effect worth knowing

`resume-run` in a worktree wrote its artifacts to the MAIN checkout's `data/store.json`, not the worktree's copy (the
copy was untouched; main gained a full copy of the project per arm). **INFERRED**: the store path is resolved by walking
up to the directory holding a real `.git` folder, and a worktree's `.git` is a file. Modules did load from the worktree
(`node_modules/@cml/*` resolves inside it), so the arms ran the pinned code.
